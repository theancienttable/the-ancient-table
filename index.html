Questions

Before you take your seat

Is this a cookbook or a history book?

It is both. Each chapter combines a food-history narrative, an explanation of the surviving evidence, a practical modern recreation, and an experience section that connects the meal to ancient life.

Are the recipes historically authentic?

The book never claims that incomplete evidence is a complete ancient recipe. Every chapter includes an evidence label and reconstruction status explaining what is documented and what a modern kitchen must supply.

Will I need rare ingredients?

Some chapters introduce less familiar ingredients, but the recreations include practical modern substitutions whenever possible. Safety and allergy notes are included throughout.

What format will I receive?

You will receive the complete 209-page English-language book as a PDF that can be opened on a phone, tablet, or computer.
